How we keep it honest

Sound attracts big claims. Discover takes the questions seriously and lets you check every answer, whatever you believe going in.

Every equation shown

Each interactive shows the formula it computes, with units you can check by hand.

Sources cited

Claims point to the paper, book or measurement they rest on, with its date and its limits.

What the model leaves out

Every lesson ends with its assumptions and what a real instrument, room or ear adds.

Predict, then test

Commit to a guess first, then hear and measure the answer with your own ears.

An evidence lab

Popular claims, such as 432 Hz tuning and the solfeggio frequencies, are graded supported, untested or contradicted, with the evidence for each.

No healing claims

Xymatics describes sound. It makes no health, healing or wellness claims, and neither do its lessons.

Planned lessons 21 lessons in four modules

Module 01

Waves & resonance

6 lessons
  1. Wave sandbox Frequency, amplitude, phase, wavelength and speed, each with visible units.
  2. Superposition studio Add waves and compare the sum with its parts, from reinforcement to cancellation.
  3. Traveling vs standing waves Moving energy and fixed nodes side by side, with boundaries and reflection made explicit.
  4. Driven resonance explorer Sweep a drive through a damped oscillator and read amplitude, phase lag, bandwidth and Q.
  5. Damping and ringdown Long and short decays after a pluck: stored energy, dissipation and perceived sustain.
  6. Beats under a microscope Nearby frequencies and their envelope, kept separate from musical tempo.
Module 02

Fourier, timbre & tuning

6 lessons
  1. Fourier building blocks Build a waveform from sine waves and compare its trace with their amplitudes and phases.
  2. Time–frequency tradeoff Why a short analysis window locates events and a long one separates nearby pitches.
  3. Harmonic series microscope Partial frequencies and amplitudes, from harmonic instruments to inharmonic bells and plates.
  4. Tuning systems comparison Equal temperament, just intonation, Pythagorean and historical temperaments, in numbers and in sound.
  5. Timbre and consonance explorer Change the partials with the interval held fixed; compare predicted roughness with your own rating.
  6. Commas and near coincidences Why chains of simple ratios do not quite close after octave folding.
Module 03

Scales, units & loudness

4 lessons
  1. Frequency scale ladder Audible sound, ultrasound, infrared, radio and slow cycles, each on its own physical scale.
  2. Sound pressure and loudness Digital level, calibrated pressure, decibels and perceived loudness, kept distinct.
  3. Tempo-to-frequency bridge Beats per minute as events per second, not as an audio pitch.
  4. Unit and scale audit Check units, conversions, logarithmic scales and range limits before trusting a connection.
Module 04

Evidence lab

5 lessons
  1. Claim and source ledger Each claim with its source, evidence type, date, limits and review status.
  2. Mechanism map Lay out a proposed causal chain and mark which links are measured and which are missing.
  3. Correlation and confounding Alternative explanations, and what different study designs can actually establish.
  4. Evidence revision history Corrections and changed interpretations recorded without erasing how a conclusion evolved.
  5. Analogy boundary cards What musical, mechanical and quantum examples share mathematically, and where the physics differs.
