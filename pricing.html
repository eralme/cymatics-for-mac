Pricing

The app is free. Premium is the lessons.

Every feature in today’s app is free, in your browser or on your Mac, with no time limit. Premium is Discover: interactive lessons on the science behind sound’s mysteries, each with its equations, sources and limits. It also adds full-length, watermark-free video export and live listening from a microphone or any input. It will be an in-app purchase in the native app for Mac, iPhone and iPad, coming to the App Store. Premium is in development, and purchasing opens when it launches.

Pricing questions

Plans, purchases and renewals

How do I buy and unlock Premium?

In the app, with Apple’s In-App Purchase. One purchase unlocks Premium on Mac, iPhone and iPad with the same Apple ID. On a new device, choose Restore purchases in the app.

Can I buy Premium today?

Not yet. Premium is in development for the native app, which is coming to the App Store for Mac, iPhone and iPad. There are no dates yet. Until then, the free app has every current feature.

How do subscriptions renew?

Monthly and yearly plans renew through your Apple ID. You manage or cancel them in your App Store account settings, like any other App Store subscription.

Is Premium Lifetime a subscription?

No. Premium Lifetime is a one-time purchase. It does not renew, and it includes future lessons and features.

What if my subscription ends?

The lessons and other Premium features lock again, and exports carry the watermark. Every free feature keeps working, and your saved notes and experiment passports stay on your device.

Which currency are the prices in?

US dollars. Prices may be adjusted to App Store price points, and the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.
